## 📌 Overview

The **Indian Bovine Recognition System** is an AI-powered breed identification platform that classifies Indian cattle and buffalo breeds from images.

The system utilizes **YOLOv8** and Deep Learning techniques to accurately recognize multiple bovine breeds, helping farmers, researchers, and livestock management organizations identify animals quickly and efficiently.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Identify cattle breeds from images
- Identify buffalo breeds from images
- Assist farmers in breed verification
- Support livestock management and breeding programs
- Promote AI adoption in agriculture
